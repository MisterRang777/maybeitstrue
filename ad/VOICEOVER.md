# WHERE'D YOU GET THAT — voice over
Timed to the delivered 30s master. Off-screen narration: nobody speaks on
camera, so the VO never has to match a mouth.

**Budget:** 35 words across 26s of picture. ~10.5s of speech, ~15s of air.
That ratio is the point — a 30s spot that talks for 25s of it sounds like a
radio ad. Every silence below is deliberate; do not fill them.

---

## SCRIPT A — "Nobody told me" (recommended)
First person, matches the film's POV. The card becomes the answer to the
question the last line asks.

| In | Over | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 0:01.0 | box on the mat | "Nobody told me about this one." |
| — | *tape, tissue* | *(silence — 2s)* |
| 0:05.9 | fingers in the fleece | "I saw it on someone. Once." |
| 0:08.6 | head through the collar | "Couldn't tell you the brand." |
| 0:10.0 | hands on the hem | "Couldn't tell you why." |
| 0:12.2 | the mirror | "Just knew." |
| — | *hood up, out the door, feet* | *(silence — 3.5s, let the music take it)* |
| 0:19.8 | four on the wall | "Wore it out." |
| 0:21.8 | hand on the sleeve | "Took four minutes." |
| 0:24.0 | his face | "Now he wants one." |
| — | *flash* | *(silence)* |
| 0:26.0 | **MAYBE IT'S TRUE** | *(silence — the card answers it)* |

**Read direction:** flat, unimpressed, close to the mic. Someone telling a mate
this, not performing it. No smile in the voice until "Now he wants one" — and
even there, only just. Never sell the last line; throw it away.

## Casting
Young, regional, unpolished — a real accent beats a neutral one. Slightly too
close to the mic, a little breath and mouth noise left in. The disposable-camera
picture is amateur on purpose; a glossy announcer voice will fight it and the
whole thing will read as an ad instead of a story.

**Avoid:** the deep cinematic trailer voice, and anything that sounds like it is
reading. If the takes sound like takes, have them say it once more while walking.

## Mix against the picture
- VO sits **under** the sound design, not on top. It is a thought, not a host.
- Duck the bed ~3dB under each line, no more. Do not carve a hole.
- The 15s of silence is where the music does the work — build it hardest at
  0:15–0:20 while nobody is talking, then drop everything on shot 18.
- Nothing over the end card. Four seconds of black, type, and one held note.

## If you want it wordless instead
The cut was built to work with no VO at all, and Script A only earns its place
if the read is genuinely dry. A mediocre voice over will make this worse than
silence. Cut it both ways before you commit.
