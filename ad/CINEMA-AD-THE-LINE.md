# THE LINE — Cinema Spot
**Brand:** Maybe It's True · Premium Streetwear. Authentic Culture. Limited Drops.
**Length:** 30s (6 × 5s) · **Format:** 16:9 DCP-ready · **Dialogue:** none
**Look:** disposable camera — direct on-camera flash, 35mm grain, blown highlights, crushed shadows, green/cyan cast, orange date stamp

## The idea
The ad *is* the mechanic. Nobody in the film knows what they're queuing for.
They queue because someone else is queuing. At the end there is exactly one
garment. Artificial demand, dramatized literally and without a word of dialogue —
the audience gets the joke before the logo lands.

**Tagline on the end card:** `NOBODY KNOWS WHY. MAYBE IT'S TRUE.`

## Why it works in a cinema
- Wordless — no subtitles, no dub, plays in any territory.
- Flash-heavy contrast reads huge on a 20m screen; grain gives the frame texture
  digital ads lack.
- Silence → crowd noise → hard cut to black is a room-quieting sound design move.
- Scarcity is stated visually (one hanger), never claimed in copy.

## Sound design
- 0–10s: near-silence, room tone, one distant car, shoe scuff.
- 10–20s: crowd murmur builds, phone shutters, a low sub-bass swell.
- 20–25s: everything ducks. Roller door rattle. A single hanger scrape.
- 25s: hard cut to black on a flash pop. 3s of silence under the logo.
